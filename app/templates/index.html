How it works

  1. You describe the problemTypos and informal language are fine. Personal numbers are masked before anything is stored.
  2. It is routed to a departmentA deep learning classifier picks the office and the urgency.
  3. You get an answer from policyRetrieval-augmented generation grounds the reply in official documents.
  4. Staff follow up when neededFormal, urgent or unclear reports go to a person, with a response target.

What you can report

Academic

Exam remarks, timetable and assessment clashes, module access.

Finance

Fees, NSFAS and bursary allowances, refunds, registration blocks.

ICT

Wi-Fi, passwords and locked accounts, email, the learning system.

Facilities

Repairs, cleanliness, lecture venues and safety hazards on campus.

Hostel

Residence places, room maintenance, water and electricity.

When staff respond

Each report gets an urgency level, which sets how soon a staff member responds.

  • Criticalwithin 4 hours
  • Highwithin 24 hours
  • Mediumwithin 3 working days
  • Lowwithin 5 working days

Check on a report

Send your reference number in the chat, for example CV-2026-000123, and CampusVoice replies with its status and any note from staff.
