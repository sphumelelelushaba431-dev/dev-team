# Residence allocation
Residence places are allocated according to academic merit, financial need and distance from home. Students apply for residence through the student portal. Allocation results are published before registration, and students on the waiting list are offered places as they become available.

# Residence maintenance
Maintenance problems in residences, such as broken geysers, no hot water, broken windows, faulty locks or leaking pipes, are reported to the residence warden or through the residence maintenance log. No hot water or a broken lock is treated as urgent and should be fixed within 24 hours. Other repairs are completed within 5 working days. If a repair is not completed in time, the student may escalate to the Residence Life manager.

# Room changes and roommate conflicts
Requests to change rooms are made to the residence warden. Roommate conflicts should first be discussed with the house committee, which can mediate. If mediation fails, the warden may approve a room change when a suitable room is available.

# Water and electricity outages
Residence-wide water or electricity outages are reported to the warden, who logs them with Facilities Management. During extended outages, the university communicates alternative arrangements through the residence notice board and student email.

# Visitors and rules
Visitors must sign in at the residence entrance and leave by the time set in the residence rules. Noise must be kept to a minimum after 22:00 on weekdays. Breaches of residence rules are dealt with by the house committee and the warden.

# Security in residences
Access to residences is controlled by student cards. Lost cards must be reported to the warden immediately so that access can be cancelled. Safety concerns, theft or harassment in residences must be reported to the warden and Campus Protection Services.

# Private accommodation
Students in accredited private accommodation should report maintenance problems to the landlord first. Unresolved problems in accredited buildings can be reported to the university's Off-Campus Accommodation office, which inspects accredited properties.
