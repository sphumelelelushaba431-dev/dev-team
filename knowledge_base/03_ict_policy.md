# Network and Wi-Fi access
Students connect to the campus Wi-Fi network using their student number and network password. Wi-Fi coverage is available in lecture venues, libraries and student residences. Persistent connection drops in a specific building should be logged with the ICT Service Desk, stating the building, room or floor and the time the problem occurs.

# Passwords and account access
Students can reset their network and email password through the self-service password portal using their registered cellphone number. If the account is locked after several failed attempts, it unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or the ICT Service Desk can unlock it with proof of identity.

# Learning management system (Moodle)
Course materials, assignment submissions and online tests are on the learning management system. If a student cannot access a module on the learning management system, the student should first confirm that they are registered for the module, then log a call with the ICT Service Desk. When the system is down close to a submission deadline, ICT confirms the outage so that lecturers can grant extensions.

# Student email
Every registered student receives a university email account. Official communication is sent only to this address. Problems with sending or receiving email should be logged with the ICT Service Desk.

# Computer labs and printing
Computer labs are open on weekdays from 07:30 to 22:00 during the semester. Faulty computers or printers should be reported to the lab assistant or the ICT Service Desk with the lab and machine number. Printing credit is loaded through the student portal.

# ICT Service Desk
The ICT Service Desk handles all technology faults. Calls are logged online or at the walk-in desk in the library. Standard calls are resolved within 2 working days, and outages affecting many students are prioritised.
