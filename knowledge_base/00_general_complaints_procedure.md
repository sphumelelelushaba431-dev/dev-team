# Student complaints procedure
These sample policies stand in for the institution's own documents. Replace them with your university's real syllabi, rules and administrative PDFs before deployment. Every complaint receives a reference number. Students can track the status of a complaint with that reference at any time.

# Response targets
Critical matters that affect a student's safety are referred to Campus Protection Services and the relevant office immediately, and staff respond within 4 hours. High urgency matters, such as registration blocks or deadlines within 48 hours, receive a response within 24 hours. Medium urgency matters receive a response within 3 working days. Low urgency matters and general queries receive a response within 5 working days.

# Escalation
If a complaint is not resolved within the response target, the student may escalate it to the head of the department responsible. If it is still not resolved, the student may lodge a formal complaint with the Dean of Students, who must respond in writing within 10 working days. Formal complaints must describe the problem, the dates involved and the steps already taken.

# Safety and wellbeing
For emergencies on campus, contact Campus Protection Services immediately. For crime, call SAPS on 10111. For medical emergencies, call 10177. Students experiencing emotional distress can contact the Student Counselling and Health Centre, which offers free and confidential support, or the South African Depression and Anxiety Group (SADAG) helpline on 0800 567 567.

# Privacy
Complaints are handled in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Personal information is used only to resolve the complaint and is shared only with staff who need it. Student numbers, ID numbers and contact details are masked in the complaints analytics dashboard.
