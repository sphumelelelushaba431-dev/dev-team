# Lecturer and teaching concerns
Concerns about teaching, a lecturer's conduct, missed lectures or unclear assessment criteria should first be raised with the lecturer. If the matter is not resolved within 5 working days, the student may raise it with the class representative and then the Head of Department. Lecturers must respond to student emails within 3 working days during the semester.

# Timetable and venue clashes
Timetable clashes between registered modules must be reported to the Faculty Office within the first two weeks of the semester. The Faculty Office will propose an alternative class group or a timetable change. Test and exam clashes must be reported at least 10 working days before the assessment date.

# Marks and results
Marks for tests and assignments must be released within 15 working days of submission. If a mark is missing or incorrect on the student portal, the student should contact the lecturer with proof of submission. Final results are published on the student portal after the examination board meets.

# Exam remarking and reviews
A student may apply for a remark of a final examination script within 5 working days of the results being published. The application is made at the Faculty Office and a remark fee applies, which is refunded if the final mark changes by 5 percent or more. A student may also apply to view their script before applying for a remark.

# Supplementary and special exams
Students who obtain a final mark between 45 and 49 percent qualify for a supplementary examination. Students who missed an examination because of illness must submit a medical certificate within 3 working days to apply for a special examination. Supplementary and special examination dates are published on the faculty notice board and portal.

# Assignment submission and extensions
Assignments are submitted on the learning management system before the deadline. Extensions may be granted by the lecturer for documented illness, bereavement or system outages confirmed by ICT. Late submissions without an approved extension may be penalised according to the module guide.

# Academic exclusion and appeals
Students who do not meet minimum progression requirements may be academically excluded. An appeal against exclusion must be submitted to the Faculty Board within 10 working days of the exclusion letter, with supporting documents.
