# Maintenance requests
Broken lights, leaking taps, damaged furniture, faulty air conditioning and blocked toilets in academic buildings are reported to the Facilities Management helpdesk. The report should include the building, floor and room number. Routine repairs are completed within 5 working days and urgent repairs within 24 hours.

# Lecture venues
Problems with lecture venues, such as broken projectors, too few chairs or poor ventilation, should be reported by the class representative or lecturer to Facilities Management. Venue overcrowding is reported to the Faculty Office so that a larger venue can be allocated.

# Safety hazards
Hazards such as exposed electrical wiring, gas smells, fire risks, flooding or broken handrails must be reported immediately to Campus Protection Services and Facilities Management. Affected areas are cordoned off until they are made safe. Do not attempt to fix electrical or gas faults yourself.

# Accessibility
Students with disabilities who encounter broken lifts, blocked ramps or inaccessible venues should report this to Facilities Management and the Disability Unit. Accessibility faults are treated as urgent.

# Cleaning and hygiene
Cleaning concerns in toilets, lecture venues and common areas are reported to Facilities Management. Toilets in academic buildings are cleaned at least twice a day.

# Parking and transport
Student parking is available in designated areas with a valid parking disc. Campus shuttle timetables are published on the portal, and complaints about shuttle delays are handled by the Transport office within Facilities Management.

# Campus security
Campus Protection Services patrols all campuses 24 hours a day. Theft, suspicious behaviour or threats should be reported to Campus Protection Services immediately.
