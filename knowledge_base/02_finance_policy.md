# Tuition fees and payment deadlines
The minimum initial payment must be made before registration. The balance of tuition fees is payable in instalments, with the final instalment due by 30 September. Students with outstanding fees after the final due date may be blocked from viewing results and from re-registering until the account is settled or an arrangement is made.

# Fee statements and account queries
Students can download an up-to-date fee statement from the student portal. Queries about incorrect charges, missing payments or duplicate debits should be sent to the Student Finance helpdesk with proof of payment. Finance resolves account queries within 5 working days.

# Bursaries and NSFAS funding
Students funded by NSFAS or an external bursary must sign their funding agreement before their allowances can be paid. If an allowance has not been paid, the student should first check the funding status on the NSFAS portal, then contact the Financial Aid office with their student number. Allowances for accommodation, meals and learning materials are paid monthly once registration and the funding agreement are confirmed.

# Allowance delays and food support
Where allowances are delayed for reasons outside the student's control, the Financial Aid office can refer students to the campus food support programme while the payment is processed. Students in urgent financial distress should contact the Dean of Students office.

# Refunds and credit balances
Students who cancel their registration before the published cancellation date may receive a partial refund of tuition fees. Credit balances on a student account can be refunded on request, after funders have been reimbursed. Refund requests are processed within 20 working days.

# Payment arrangements
Students who cannot pay the full balance may apply for a payment arrangement at the Student Finance office. An approved arrangement lifts the results block while payments are kept up to date.
